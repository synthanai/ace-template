## The Glass Hood

Underneath this interface lies a 11-system biological harness designed for enterprise-grade execution. As a Sovereign Driver, you do not need to understand the mechanics of these systems to command them. You only need to know they are humming beneath you.

1. **Contain** (`01_sandbox/`): The Skin. Protects the host environment.
2. **Structure** (`02_registry/`): The Skeleton. Defines tools and schemas.
3. **Move** (`03_execution/`): The Muscle. Executes deterministic logic.
4. **Remember** (`04_session_logs/`): The Nervous System. Immutable state tracking.
5. **Signal** (`05_dynamic_state/`): The Endocrine System. Injects your Element.
6. **Pump** (`06_outer_loop/`): The Heart. Drives the Observe/Act/Evaluate cycle.
7. **Defend** (`07_permission_gates/`): The Immune System. Halts destructive actions.
8. **Breathe** (`08_lifecycle_hooks/`): The Lungs. Pre/post validation.
9. **Digest** (`09_token_budget/`): The Digestive Tract. Absorbs massive contexts.
10. **Prune** (`10_context_pruner/`): The Excretory System. Evicts dead memory.
11. **Spawn** (`11_child_agents/`): The Reproductive System. Spins sub-threads.

## The Ignition

This organism is currently running on **Factory Default**. It has a body, but it has no soul. 

To wake the organism:
1. Open `identity.yaml`.
2. Change `element: DEFAULT` to your Day 1 Elemental Spec (Fire, Water, Air, Earth, or Space).
3. Save the file. 

The 11-system harness will instantly detect the shift, re-routing its execution rhythm to match your cognitive profile.

## The Steering Wheel

Once the ignition is set, the organism works in the background. The engine is intentionally hidden inside the `.agent/` folder to leave your root directory pristine.

Your root folders (`1-concepts/`, `2-research/`, `3-projects/`, `4-operations/`) belong entirely to you. They are your clean workspace.

To steer the organism, you trigger the **ARC (Anatomy of Execution)** workflows from the `.agent/workflows/` directory. These 12 hyper-specialized commands are built to solve specific cognitive problems. Run them in your workspace, and the invisible engine will execute.
